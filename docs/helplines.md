# Helplines

The list lives in `tools/layout.py` (`HELPLINES`) and is the only place numbers are defined. Nothing else on the site should show a phone number.

Last checked: November 2026, from public government sources.

| Service | Number | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Emergency Response Support System | 112 | Police, fire, ambulance |
| Tele-MANAS | 14416 or 1-800-891-4416 | Free national mental health support, 24 hours, many languages |
| Women Helpline | 181 | Women facing violence |
| Childline | 1098 | Children in need of care and protection |
| Elderline | 14567 | Senior citizens |
| Cyber crime | 1930, and cybercrime.gov.in | Online fraud and cyber crime |

## Re-verification

Re-verify every number against the official government source before launch and every 6 months after. Record the date and the person who checked in this file. If a number cannot be confirmed, remove it rather than guess.
