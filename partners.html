Work with us

For partners

Wellbeing grows in communities. We hope to work with the organisations people already trust, after checking each one carefully.

Organisations appear here only after verification. Partner listings open district by district.

Who we hope to work with

  • Schools

    CBSE, ICSE and state board schools, for counsellors, teachers and parent groups.

  • Colleges and hostels

    Student welfare cells and counsellors.

  • Anganwadis and ASHA networks

    Frontline workers who meet mothers and young children at home.

  • PHCs and CHCs

    Primary and community health centres.

  • District Mental Health Programme teams

    Clinics and outreach in every district.

  • One Stop Centres (Sakhi)

    Integrated support for women facing violence.

  • Child Welfare Committees and DCPUs

    Statutory bodies for children in need of care and protection.

  • NGOs

    Non-profits doing community mental health and protection work.

  • Self-help groups and panchayats

    Women's collectives and village bodies.

  • Resident welfare associations

    Neighbourhood groups in towns and cities.

  • Psychologists

    With RCI registration.

  • Psychiatrists

    With NMC or state medical council registration.

What partnership involves

  • Receiving referrals from SAATHI and replying to the person in the way they asked.
  • Telling us honestly about your capacity, languages and service area.
  • Following child-protection and confidentiality rules, including POCSO reporting duties.
  • Keeping people's information safe under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023.
  • Taking part in reviews, and telling us when something goes wrong.

How we verify a partner

We will not list or refer to anyone who has not completed every step.

  1. Registration and credentials

    We check the organisation's registration, and individual credentials, for example, RCI for psychologists and NMC for psychiatrists.

  2. Safeguarding policy

    A written policy for protecting children and vulnerable adults, with named people responsible.

  3. Service area and eligibility

    Where you work, who you serve, in which languages, and at what cost.

  4. Data-sharing agreement

    A written agreement on how information is shared and protected, in line with the DPDP Act 2023.

  5. Periodic review

    Regular check-ins, and listening to feedback from the people referred.
