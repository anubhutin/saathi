Where we are going

Roadmap

From a website to a trusted service across India, in five phases. We will not skip the steps that keep people safe.

Five phases

Where we are now: Phase 2. The website, library, age pathways and check-in are open to everyone. Human support through SAATHI is being set up with verified partners, starting with pilot districts.

  1. Phase 1: product foundation

    Finalise the user research, service model, age pathways, content standards, design system and first languages.

  2. Phase 2: functional digital experience (where we are now)

    Build the responsive website, content library, guided journeys, multilingual navigation, accessibility and privacy-conscious browsing.

  3. Phase 3: human support pilot

    Verify referral partners, set up professional workflows, and build consent-based referrals, appointments, follow-ups and safeguarding procedures.

  4. Phase 4: pilot evaluation

    Test with a small, diverse group of users and measure accessibility, usefulness, referral completion, trust and safety.

  5. Phase 5: responsible expansion

    Add languages, locations, content partners and service networks, and improve accessibility, based on what the pilot shows.

How we will know whether SAATHI is working

Not by page views alone. We will look at whether people felt safe, understood and able to reach help.

AreaExample indicators
ReachUnique visitors, age-group reach, language use
EngagementResource completion, repeat visits, activity participation
AccessibilityLow-bandwidth performance, audio usage, accessibility test results
Help-seekingVoluntary support requests and completed referrals
ContinuityAppointments attended, follow-ups completed
TrustUser-reported comfort, understanding and perceived safety
QualityContent review completion, partner verification
OutcomesAppropriate, ethically collected user-reported changes over time

We will use aggregated, privacy-preserving reporting. We will not make clinical outcome claims from simple engagement data.
