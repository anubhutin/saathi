Why SAATHI

Help should feel easier to reach.

Many women and girls carry difficult things quietly. Stigma, judgement, privacy concerns, limited mobility and not knowing where to turn can make asking for help feel out of reach. SAATHI meets them in a familiar digital space—with useful everyday content first, and a gentle path to human support when they want it.
