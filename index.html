You don't need to be in crisis to be here

Needing help is not a weakness, and it is not "pagal". It is human.

Many of us wait until things are unbearable before we ask for anything. SAATHI does not ask you to prove that you are struggling.

In many homes, the first worry is not "what is wrong with me?" but "log kya kahenge?" What will the neighbours say, what will the relatives think, will it spoil a rishta. So we stay quiet, and carry it alone.

We know the other things that get in the way. One phone shared by the whole family. Needing permission to leave the house, or no one to go with. The cost of a clinic visit and a day's wages lost. Hours of travel, because there may be no counsellor within reach outside a city.

So come for a story, a few minutes of calm, an answer to a question, or a way to find help. Nobody asks for your name, a label or a form. Nothing you type here is sent or stored, and the shared phone tips show how to keep your reading private. You stay in charge, and if you want to talk to someone, we will help you find a real person, only with your consent.
